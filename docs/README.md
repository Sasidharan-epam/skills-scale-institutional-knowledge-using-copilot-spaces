# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative lifecycle: **Initiation** confirms the business need and success metrics; **Planning** turns approved work into a prioritized backlog, release plan, responsibilities, dependencies, estimates, and a Definition of Done; **Execution and Tracking** use project boards, daily standups, reviews, testing, and metrics to deliver increments; **Risk and Communication** maintain transparency through registers, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths; **Release and Deployment** standardize pre-release checks, production deployment, rollback, and incident handling; and **Retrospectives** turn learnings into owned, tracked improvements.

## How the Documents Fit Together

Start with the [Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md) for the principles, lifecycle, roles, and communication rhythm. Use [Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md) to validate and authorize the work, then follow [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md) to build the actionable delivery plan. [Execution and Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) and [Risk Management and Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) guide delivery and transparency. [Release and Deployment](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) covers production readiness and deployment, while [Retrospective and Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) closes the lifecycle and feeds learning back into future work.
